Tunable Parameters

Raster Detection:
• Adaptive threshold block size: 15 (odd number, 11-31)
• Canny thresholds: 50-150 (low-high)
• Hough minimum line length: 30px
• Collinearity angle tolerance: 5°

• Proximity threshold: 30px
